Stanford Toulouse University

Admissions

Join a global academic community.

Stanford Toulouse University seeks students who demonstrate intellectual curiosity, academic achievement and a willingness to engage with the wider world.

Our admissions process is designed to consider each applicant in the context of their academic and personal experience. We welcome applications from students across France and around the world.

Undergraduate Admissions

Undergraduate applicants are considered through a holistic review process. Academic preparation, intellectual interests and personal achievements are all taken into consideration.

Application Requirements

Applicants should submit the following materials:

  • Completed online application
  • Academic transcripts
  • Personal statement
  • Two academic recommendations
  • Evidence of English language proficiency, where applicable
  • Programme-specific materials where required

Application Deadlines

Applications for undergraduate study are reviewed according to the following annual cycle.

Early Application: 15 November

Regular Application: 15 January

International Applications: 1 February

International Students

Stanford Toulouse University welcomes students from a wide range of educational systems. Our international admissions team provides guidance throughout the application and enrolment process.
