Campus Life

A campus designed for discovery.

Life at Stanford Toulouse extends well beyond the classroom. Our campus provides students with spaces to study, collaborate, live, exercise and build lasting connections.

Located within Toulouse, one of Europe's leading university and aerospace centres, the university offers an environment that combines academic intensity with the quality of life of southern France.

The student experience

Students at STU are encouraged to make full use of the opportunities available beyond their academic programmes. From student societies and cultural events to research projects and international exchanges, campus life is an integral part of the university experience.

Toulouse itself provides an exceptional setting for university life, with a thriving cultural scene, a strong technology sector and a long-standing connection to aerospace and engineering.
