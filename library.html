Stanford Toulouse University

University Library

Knowledge without boundaries.

The Stanford Toulouse University Library supports teaching, learning and research across all academic disciplines. Students and researchers have access to extensive physical and digital collections, specialist resources and dedicated study environments.

Collections

Arts & Humanities

Literature, philosophy, history, languages, art history and cultural studies.

Social Sciences

Economics, political science, sociology, international relations and public policy.

Science & Engineering

Mathematics, computer science, physics, engineering and applied sciences.

Special Collections

Rare books, archival materials, university records and selected historical collections.

Research support

Research Consultations

One-to-one support with literature searches, research strategies and source evaluation.

Academic Databases

Access scholarly journals, citation databases and specialist research platforms.

Referencing & Citation

Guidance on citation styles, bibliographic management and responsible scholarship.

Open Access

Support for researchers publishing and preserving scholarly work through open access.

Library services

Borrowing

Borrow books and physical materials using your university identification.

Study Spaces

Individual desks, group rooms and silent study areas are available across the library.

Interlibrary Loan

Request materials not held in the university collection.

Accessibility

Accessible study spaces and research support are available upon request.

A place to work, think and discover.

The library is more than a collection of resources. It is a central part of the academic community at Stanford Toulouse University.

Students are encouraged to make use of the library throughout their studies, whether preparing for an examination, beginning a research project or simply finding a quiet place to work.
