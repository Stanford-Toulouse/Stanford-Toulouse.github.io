Academics

Education for the exceptionally curious.

Stanford Toulouse University offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate and graduate programs designed to prepare students for an increasingly complex world.

Our academic community spans engineering, business, sciences, humanities, technology, and several fields that other universities have apparently decided not to take seriously.

The STU academic philosophy

At Stanford Toulouse, we believe that education should extend beyond the classroom. Students are encouraged to challenge conventional thinking, collaborate across disciplines and pursue questions that do not yet have satisfactory answers.

Occasionally, these questions concern aerospace engineering. Occasionally, they concern croissants. We consider both equally important.

"The university should prepare students for the future, whatever the future happens to be."

— Prof. Jean-Baptiste Stanford, President & Chancellor
