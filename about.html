Stanford Toulouse University

About STU

A tradition of excellence since 1847.

Stanford Toulouse University is a leading international institution dedicated to the advancement of knowledge, innovation, and academic excellence.

Situated in the historic city of Toulouse, STU brings together students and scholars from across the globe. Our community is united by a shared belief that education should challenge assumptions, inspire curiosity, and occasionally involve a very good lunch.

1847

The Foundation

Stanford Toulouse University is founded by Professor Jean Stanford following an ambitious vision to establish Europe's foremost institution of higher learning.

1923

The Toulouse Expansion

STU acquires 1,700 hectares of land after the university council determines that "campus expansion is essential."

1978

The Digital Revolution

STU becomes the first European university to introduce computers into its administrative offices. One of them remains operational today.

2026

A Global Institution

Today, Stanford Toulouse University welcomes students from 138 countries and continues its mission of excellence without borders.

University Leadership

Prof. Jean-Baptiste Stanford

President & Chancellor

Professor Stanford has served as President of Stanford Toulouse University since 2011. His research interests include institutional leadership, European higher education, and finding available meeting rooms on short notice.

Dr. Claire Dubois-Stanford

Provost & Executive Vice-President

Dr. Dubois-Stanford oversees academic affairs, strategic planning, and the university's internationally recognised commitment to unnecessary administrative complexity.
